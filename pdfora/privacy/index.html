PDFora — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 · Developer: annidev · Contact: anggityogipratama14@gmail.com

PDFora processes your documents on your device, and it shows ads to stay free.

Your files

Every PDF you open, edit, scan, convert or protect is processed on this device. The content of your PDFs is never uploaded or sent to any server, and never to the ad network. PDFora has no account. Files you import are copied into the app's own storage; files you open are read through Android's system file picker, so PDFora needs no broad storage permission.

Ads

PDFora is free and contains ads. They are provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and appear as a banner on the main screens and, now and then, as a full-screen ad after you finish a task. Ads are not shown while you read, edit or annotate a document.

To load and show ads, PDFora uses the internet, and Google AdMob may collect and use this data from your device: your Android Advertising ID and other device identifiers, your IP address (a general location can be estimated from it), how you interact with ads (such as views and taps) and diagnostic information about the ads. Google uses it to choose and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud, and may share it with its advertising partners. This data does not include the content or the names of your PDFs. PDFora has no other analytics of its own.

Your choices: in some regions (for example the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) PDFora asks for your consent before it requests any ad; if you do not agree, no ad is requested. Where this applies, you can review or change your choice in Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID, or limit ad personalisation, in your Android settings (usually under Privacy > Ads or Google > Ads; the names differ between devices).

If you are offline, or an ad cannot be loaded, nothing is shown and PDFora works as usual. PDFora is not directed at children. How Google handles advertising data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Permissions

PDFora asks for network access (internet and network state) and the Advertising ID permission, only so that ads can be loaded. It requests no storage or camera permission.

Google components used on the device

  • Text recognition (OCR) uses Google ML Kit with a model bundled inside the app, so the text is read on your device and the pages are not sent anywhere.
  • Scan Document opens Google's document scanner through Google Play services on your device. The first use may need an internet connection for Google to provide the scanner component. The scanned pages are handed back to PDFora, which builds the PDF itself.

Fonts

When you type a letter that a PDF's own font does not have, PDFora uses Liberation Sans (SIL Open Font License), which is bundled inside the app. Nothing is downloaded for this.

Things you start yourself

  • Share sends the file to the app you pick in the Android share sheet.
  • Print sends the document to the print service you pick.

What those apps and services do with a file is governed by their own policies.

Backup

PDFora turns off Android's automatic app backup, so the files in the app's own storage are not copied to your Google account. If you uninstall the app or move to a new phone, those copies are not restored; keep your originals where you saved them.

You can delete the app's temporary files at any time with Settings > Clear Cache.
